NibTeXa is a notebook for writing by hand on an Android tablet, made for maths, science and engineering notes.

• Write with math, text, drawing and scratch pens, on blank, ruled or grid paper
• Handwriting turns into LaTeX on your tablet, and you check it before it's used
• Draw block diagrams, signal flow graphs, circuits and rough graphs: each stroke becomes a clean, attached part
• Graph equations on the page
• Export to PDF, LaTeX and Markdown, in columns and with your own margins
• Import LaTeX, Markdown and PDFs to write on
• Automatic backups to a folder you choose
• Commit and push to any Git server you set up

No accounts and no ads.

Please note:
• Reading handwritten words uses Google ML Kit, which sends usage metrics to Google. Your handwriting stays on your tablet.
• Reading handwritten maths needs a separate model of about 1.25 GB, which you can download from its authors in Settings. It takes about 1.7 GB of memory while in use, so a tablet with 6 GB or more is best.
• This is an early version.

NibTeXa is free software under the GNU GPL, version 3, with its source at https://github.com/Iamthebarrow/nibtexa
